About

M.Sc. Physics and B.Ed. graduate who has taught Physics in CBSE schools since 2017, and is now preparing to handle classes 11 and 12. I have prepared students for CBSE board examinations and competitive tests, and mentored them for national-level science competitions. My focus is conceptual clarity and problem-solving, backed by practical demonstration and continuous assessment.
